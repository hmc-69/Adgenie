## Research notes and status policy

Facts above are limited to the linked official publisher pages. A public page, downloadable file, API credential, or technically accessible record does not prove that AdGenie has permission to store or use it for machine-learning training. The Meta terms link was identified, but its terms and application to model training were not adjudicated here. The Criteo Uplift page explicitly states CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; this register does not offer legal advice.

- `unreviewed`: candidate facts or terms have not been reviewed enough to permit collection or use.
- `approved_for_review`: a lead may enter documented human review; this is not permission to download or train.
- `rejected`: known task mismatch or a documented restriction makes it unsuitable for this task as currently scoped.

No source is currently `approved_for_review` or approved for production. Search for sources containing campaign/ad creative text only as leads; the nine archetype labels will require independent human annotation under [`REAL_DATA_ANNOTATION_GUIDE.md`](REAL_DATA_ANNOTATION_GUIDE.md). Do not use campaign delivery or conversion fields as a substitute for these labels.
